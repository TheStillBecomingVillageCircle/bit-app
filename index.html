BIT's guiding principles

01Both people matter. The same care and standards apply to each partner.
02Feelings are allowed. The goal is not forced positivity; it is honest communication without cruelty or threats.
03Private means private. Journals and raw reflections are never shared automatically.
04Consent comes first. Each partner chooses whether to join and what they share.
